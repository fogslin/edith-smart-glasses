EDITH — DIY AI smart glasses on a XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense

A thumb-sized ESP32-S3 board with an onboard camera and PDM microphone lives in a pod on the right temple. A tiny OLED + beam-splitter gives you a heads-up display, a MAX98357A amp drives a speaker or bone-conduction transducer, a copper-foil touch strip gives you swipe/tap gestures, and a MAX30102-class sensor shows your heart rate. The heavy lifting (Whisper, a vision LLM such as Grok, text-to-speech, Tesla control) runs on a small backend server on your PC, a Raspberry Pi or a VPS.

2 · TranslateInstant translationMic → Whisper → LLM translate → HUD caption + spoken TTS.
3 · MusicMusic playbackWi-Fi audio stream from the backend → I²S amp → speaker / bone conductor.
4 · TextText & notificationsPush captions to the HUD from a phone shortcut or Home Assistant.
5 · "AR"Scene labelsCamera frame → vision LLM → up to 4 labels on the HUD (2D overlay, not SLAM).
6 · GrokGrok connectionxAI's OpenAI-compatible API at https://api.x.ai/v1.
7 · Custom AISwappable brainPersonality prompt file + any OpenAI-compatible provider (xAI, OpenAI, local Ollama…).
8 · HeartHeart-rate on HUDMAX30102 / MAX30101 on the shared I²C bus, BPM in the HUD corner.
9 · TeslaCar controlFleet API through Tesla's signing proxy, or local BLE via tesla-control.
+ · TouchGesture strip3 copper pads: swipe = volume, tap = play/pause, double-tap = wake Edith.
This is a hobby prototype, not a product. Expect roughly 1–2 hours of active use from the default EEMB LP502030 250 mAh cell (longer with a 400–500 mAh cell, which needs a bigger pod), 2–5 s round-trip latency for AI answers, a small monochrome HUD that needs optical tuning, and a pod that is chunkier than commercial glasses. The camera board can get hot (Seeed measured 63.6 °C on the back of the board without a heat sink) — keep it insulated from skin.

Tools & consumables

Fine-tip temperature-controlled soldering iron (≈330–350 °C for leaded, ≈350–370 °C lead-free), thin rosin-core solder (0.3–0.5 mm), flux pen, tweezers, flush cutters, wire strippers for 30 AWG, multimeter, helping hands/magnifier, Kapton tape, hot-glue or UV resin, a hobby knife / rotary tool for routing channels, and (optionally) a 3D printer for pods.

Why not I²S on D0/D1/D2? The touch strip needs three touch-capable pins, and on the XIAO only D0–D5 and D8–D10 are touch-capable (GPIO1–9). D4/D5 are kept for I²C (the board's default SDA/SCL), so the I²S clocks moved to the non-touch pins D6/D7, which frees D0, D1 and D3 for the pads. D8–D10 are shared with the Sense board's microSD slot, so this build does not use the SD card — leave the slot empty.
GPIO43/44 (D6/D7) are UART0 TX/RX; the ROM bootloader prints on GPIO43 at reset, so the amp may emit a short click at power-on. The firmware's Serial uses USB-CDC instead, so this is harmless. GPIO3 (D2) is a strapping pin for JTAG source selection; it is only used as an output (amp DIN), which is fine.

4 Soldering guide

Everything uses 30 AWG silicone stranded wire (flexible enough to survive the hinge). Colour-code: red = +, black = GND, blue = SDA, yellow = SCL, green = I²S data, purple = BCLK, orange = LRC, white = touch.

  1. Prep & ESD. Remove the Sense expansion board by sliding it off sideways (Seeed: never pry vertically). Work on an ESD mat, iron at ~330–350 °C, flux on every joint.
  2. Tin the pads. Touch iron + solder to each castellated pad you will use (D0, D1, D2, D3, D4, D5, D6, D7, D8, D10, 3V3, GND) for ~1 s to leave a small dome. Tin every wire end too (strip 2 mm, twist, tin).
  3. Header vs direct-wire. Prototype on a breadboard with pin headers first. For the glasses, direct-wire to the castellated pads (no headers) to keep the pod thin. Lay the tinned wire flat on the tinned pad and reflow for 1–2 s.
  4. Battery pads (underside). The BAT pads are on the back of the XIAO. Per Seeed, the negative pad is the one closer to the USB port, the positive pad is farther from it. Solder red (via the switch) to BAT+, black to BAT−. Keep the battery disconnected (switch off / connector open) while soldering anything else.
  5. Heat-shrink & insulation. Slide 1.5 mm heat-shrink over every in-line joint (switch, resistor, LED legs) before soldering, then shrink with hot air. Cover the underside BAT pads and the board back with Kapton tape so nothing shorts against the temple or the battery.
  6. Strain relief. Put a drop of hot glue / UV resin where each wire leaves a pad. Leave a 10–15 mm service loop at the hinge so the wires bend over a large radius instead of at the joint.
  7. Continuity check. With the multimeter: BAT+ to BAT− must not beep; 3V3 to GND must not beep; each touch pad must not beep to GND. Then power from USB first, battery second.
LiPo safety: never solder directly on a cell's tabs, never short the leads (cut and insulate one wire at a time), and never let the iron touch the pouch. Use a cell with a protection circuit (the Adafruit cells listed include one).

5 Battery, power switch & charging port placement

Layout for balance

  • Right temple, front pod: XIAO ESP32-S3 + Sense board, camera folded forward through a hole at the front of the pod, status LED beside the lens (privacy indicator).
  • Left temple, rear-mid pod: the LiPo — default EEMB LP502030 (3.7 V 250 mAh, 20.5 × 31–32 × 5.3 mm, 5 g, built-in PCM, red/black leads to a 2.0 mm JST on the -PCM version: cut the JST off and solder the leads) — opposite side from the electronics so the frame doesn't tilt.
  • Power switch: SPDT slide switch on top of the left temple, in series with the battery's red lead.
  • Wire route: BAT+/BAT− run along the left temple, through the hinge with a service loop, along a groove behind the top bar (brow line) of the front frame, through the right hinge, into the pod.

USB-C charging

The XIAO has onboard Li-ion charge management: plug USB-C in and it charges the cell on the BAT pads (red charge LED flashes while charging, turns off when full). Mount the board flat against the temple with the USB-C port facing backward out of the rear of the pod so a cable plugs in from behind.

Seeed notes there is no voltage on the 5V pin when running from battery, and there is no GPIO wired to the battery for voltage sensing (you'd need a divider to a free ADC pin).

Which edge of the Sense board the camera ribbon leaves from, and whether the ribbon can fold forward when the board lies along the temple, depends on your board/camera revision — dry-fit before gluing. If it doesn't fit, rotate the board (USB-C forward) and cut a charging slot in the bottom of the pod instead.
  1. Dry-fit the board, battery and switch on the temples with Blu-Tack; check the hinges still fold and the frame sits level.
  2. Switch in series: battery red → switch common pin; switch outer pin → red wire to XIAO BAT+. Battery black → XIAO BAT− directly.
  3. Route the pair along the inside of the left temple, over the hinge with a service loop, in a groove behind the brow bar (cut with a hobby knife or 3D-print a cover), and down the right hinge.
  4. Open the USB-C slot at the back of the pod; test charging with the switch ON (the switch must be on for the cell to be connected to the charger).
  5. Expect ~1–2 h of active use with the 250 mAh EEMB cell (charge at ≤250 mA max, 4.2 V CV — the XIAO's onboard charger is well below that): Seeed lists ~110 mA with Wi-Fi active and up to ~366 mA peaks during image capture on battery for the Sense. Deep sleep between uses helps.

6 HUD display & optics

A 128×64 (0.96″) or 128×32 (0.91″) SSD1306 I²C OLED sits on a small arm above the right lens, facing down. Below it a thin 45° beam-splitter (a 50/50 plate or a small glass cube/prism) in front of the upper-outer part of the right lens reflects the image toward your eye while you still see through it.

Your eye cannot focus on something 2–3 cm away. Without optics the HUD is a blur. Put a small plano-convex lens (focal length ≈ 25–40 mm) between the OLED and the splitter, with the OLED slightly inside the focal length, so you see a virtual image that appears ~0.5–1 m away. Fine-tune the distance with spacers. Because the reflected image passes through your Rx lens after the splitter, your prescription also applies to the HUD.
  1. Wire the OLED to the shared I²C bus: VCC→3V3, GND→GND, SDA→D4 (GPIO5), SCL→D5 (GPIO6). Address 0x3C.
  2. Bench test with the firmware: the OLED shows “Hello. I'm Edith.” and the mode name.
  3. Build the optic holder (3D-print or bend thin aluminium): OLED facing down, lens below it, splitter at 45° below the lens, splitter's reflective side toward you.
  4. Fix mirroring. A single reflection flips the image. If text reads backwards, flip it in firmware (draw into a buffer and mirror, or rotate the display with display.setRotation() plus a mirrored font) — or add a second mirror.
  5. Align so the image sits ~10–15° above your straight-ahead line of sight; it should never cover your central vision while walking.

Alternative: transparent OLED modules exist but are larger and need their own driver; a single SSD1306 + splitter is the simplest and cheapest path.

7 Speaker / bone conduction (MAX98357A)

The MAX98357A is an I²S DAC + class-D amp. The ESP32-S3 sends 16 kHz, 16-bit mono PCM on its second I²S peripheral (the PDM mic uses the first).

  1. Wire: VIN→3V3, GND→GND, DIN→D2 (GPIO3), BCLK→D6 (GPIO43), LRC→D7 (GPIO44). Leave GAIN unconnected (default gain) and SD unconnected (amp on).
  2. Output: amp + / − to an 8 Ω 1 W mini speaker or a bone-conduction transducer. Mount it on the inside of the right temple just in front of the ear.
  3. Test: switch to MUSIC mode (long-press button until “MUSIC”), short-press to stream; swipe on the touch strip to change volume.
Bone conductors are quieter and need firm contact with bone; mini speakers are louder but leak sound. Running VIN from 3V3 keeps wiring simple; powering VIN from the battery gives a bit more volume.

8 Heart-rate sensor (MAX30102 / MAX30101)

The sensor shares the I²C bus with the OLED (address 0x57 vs 0x3C). The firmware uses SparkFun's MAX3010x library (checkForBeat()) and shows a rolling BPM in the HUD corner and in HEART mode.

  1. Daisy-chain from the OLED: VIN→3V3, GND, SDA, SCL. Keep the run short and twist SDA/SCL with GND if it is longer than ~15 cm.
  2. Placement: inside of the right temple pressing on the skin just in front of the ear (over the superficial temporal artery) — or on a nose pad. The sensor window must touch skin and stay still.
  3. Test: HEART mode shows “Place sensor on skin” until the IR reading passes the threshold, then BPM after a few beats.
Motion makes optical heart-rate readings noisy, and side-of-head PPG is weaker than a fingertip. Treat the number as a hobby readout, not medical data. Many breakout boards have their own I²C pull-ups; with two boards on the bus that's fine at this size.

9 Capacitive touch strip (gestures)

Three copper-foil pads in a row along the outer side of the right temple, each wired to its own touch channel: A → D0 (GPIO1/TOUCH1) front, B → D1 (GPIO2/TOUCH2) middle, C → D3 (GPIO4/TOUCH4) rear. The order in which pads light up tells the direction of a swipe.

Hardware & placement

  1. Cut pads from 6 mm copper tape with conductive adhesive: three pieces ~8 mm long, laid at 8–10 mm centre-to-centre with ~1.5–2 mm gaps. Keep the strip ≥10 mm away from the hinge and well away from the battery pod (metal and the cell's mass shift the baseline).
  2. Flush mount: route a 0.3–0.5 mm deep channel in the temple (hobby knife / rotary tool), lay the foil in it, and cover with thin vinyl, paint, or heat-shrink so it looks like a normal temple. Each extra covering layer lowers sensitivity — tune TOUCH_RATIO down (e.g. 1.06) for thicker covers.
  3. Solder a 30 AWG wire to a small tab folded at the end of each pad: tin the wire first, then touch the iron for <1 s at low heat so the adhesive doesn't lift. Alternative: stick the foil onto a small solder tab / copper-clad pad and solder to that.
  4. Keep traces short (<5 cm) and apart from each other and from the I²S wires; long parallel runs add capacitance and crosstalk. Optional: a grounded foil strip on the inside of the temple (not under the pads) reduces false triggers from your head.
  5. Calibrate: the firmware samples a baseline at boot — keep fingers off the temple while powering on. Watch the serial log (touch pad … baseline) and adjust TOUCH_RATIO.
TTP223 alternative. A TTP223 touch-IC board outputs only on/off, so one board can do taps but cannot tell swipe direction. Two TTP223 boards on two GPIOs can infer direction from which one fires first (slower: the TTP223's output response time is 60–220 ms per Seeed's Grove Touch Sensor wiki, so swipes must be deliberate). Wire VCC→3V3, GND, SIG→a free GPIO (read with digitalRead).

Firmware (integrated in the main sketch)

The gesture engine (calibrateTouch(), pollTouch(), applyGesture()) is part of edith_glasses.ino (see Firmware); it's polled in loop() and inside the audio playback loop so gestures work while music plays.

touch gesture engine (excerpt of edith_glasses.ino) 
// ---------------- capacitive touch strip ----------------
// ESP32-S3: touchRead() value RISES when touched (opposite of the original ESP32).
const uint8_t TOUCH_PINS[3] = {PIN_TOUCH_A, PIN_TOUCH_B, PIN_TOUCH_C};
uint32_t touchBase[3];
float    TOUCH_RATIO = 1.12f;          // touched if value > baseline * ratio (tune 1.05-1.30)
const uint16_t TAP_MAX_MS       = 280; // a tap is shorter than this
const uint16_t DOUBLE_GAP_MS    = 350; // second tap must start within this after the first ends
const uint16_t SWIPE_MAX_MS     = 700; // whole swipe must finish within this
const uint16_t SWIPE_MIN_STEP   = 15;  // pads must light up at least this far apart (ms)
const uint16_t TOUCH_DEBOUNCE   = 3;   // consecutive samples to change pad state

enum Gesture { G_NONE, G_SWIPE_FWD, G_SWIPE_BACK, G_TAP, G_DOUBLE_TAP };
float volumeGain = 0.6f;               // 0.0 - 1.0 software volume
bool  paused = false;

bool padState[3] = {false, false, false};
uint8_t padCount[3] = {0, 0, 0};
unsigned long padFirstOn[3];
bool gestureActive = false;
unsigned long gestureStart = 0;
unsigned long lastTapEnd = 0;
bool tapPending = false;

void calibrateTouch() {
  for (int p = 0; p < 3; p++) {
    uint64_t sum = 0;
    for (int i = 0; i < 32; i++) { sum += touchRead(TOUCH_PINS[p]); delay(2); }
    touchBase[p] = (uint32_t)(sum / 32);
    Serial.printf("touch pad %d (GPIO%d) baseline %u\n", p, TOUCH_PINS[p], touchBase[p]);
  }
}

// Call often (every loop / every audio chunk). Returns a gesture once when recognised.
Gesture pollTouch() {
  unsigned long now = millis();
  bool anyOn = false;
  for (int p = 0; p < 3; p++) {
    uint32_t v = touchRead(TOUCH_PINS[p]);
    bool raw = v > (uint32_t)(touchBase[p] * TOUCH_RATIO);
    if (raw != padState[p]) {
      if (++padCount[p] >= TOUCH_DEBOUNCE) {
        padState[p] = raw; padCount[p] = 0;
        if (raw) {
          if (!gestureActive) { gestureActive = true; gestureStart = now; for (int k = 0; k < 3; k++) padFirstOn[k] = 0; }
          if (padFirstOn[p] == 0) padFirstOn[p] = now;
        }
      }
    } else padCount[p] = 0;
    if (!raw && !padState[p]) {
      // slow drift compensation while untouched
      touchBase[p] = (touchBase[p] * 63 + v) / 64;
    }
    anyOn |= padState[p];
  }

  if (gestureActive && !anyOn) {                 // finger lifted: classify
    gestureActive = false;
    unsigned long dur = now - gestureStart;
    unsigned long a = padFirstOn[0], b = padFirstOn[1], c = padFirstOn[2];
    int lit = (a > 0) + (b > 0) + (c > 0);
    if (lit >= 2 && dur <= SWIPE_MAX_MS) {
      // order of first activation decides direction
      unsigned long first = 0, last = 0; int fi = -1, li = -1;
      for (int p = 0; p < 3; p++) if (padFirstOn[p]) {
        if (fi < 0 || padFirstOn[p] < first) { first = padFirstOn[p]; fi = p; }
        if (li < 0 || padFirstOn[p] >= last) { last = padFirstOn[p]; li = p; }
      }
      if (last - first >= SWIPE_MIN_STEP && fi != li) {
        tapPending = false;
        return (fi > li) ? G_SWIPE_FWD : G_SWIPE_BACK;   // C->A = forward, A->C = back
      }
    }
    if (dur <= TAP_MAX_MS) {
      if (tapPending && (gestureStart - lastTapEnd) <= DOUBLE_GAP_MS) { tapPending = false; return G_DOUBLE_TAP; }
      tapPending = true; lastTapEnd = now;
    }
  }
  if (tapPending && !gestureActive && now - lastTapEnd > DOUBLE_GAP_MS) { tapPending = false; return G_TAP; }
  return G_NONE;
}

void applyGesture(Gesture g, bool inAudio) {
  switch (g) {
    case G_SWIPE_FWD:  volumeGain = min(1.0f, volumeGain + 0.1f); hud("Volume " + String((int)(volumeGain * 100)) + "%", "VOL+"); break;
    case G_SWIPE_BACK: volumeGain = max(0.0f, volumeGain - 0.1f); hud("Volume " + String((int)(volumeGain * 100)) + "%", "VOL-"); break;
    case G_TAP:        if (inAudio) { paused = !paused; hud(paused ? "Paused" : "Playing", "MEDIA"); } break;
    case G_DOUBLE_TAP: wakeRequested = true; if (inAudio) stopAudio = true; break;
    default: break;
  }
}

ESPHome alternative

ESPHome's esp32_touch platform supports ESP32-S3 (GPIO1–GPIO14). This YAML uses on_multi_click for tap / double-tap on the middle pad and pad-order for swipes. Flash with setup_mode: true first to read raw values and pick thresholds. (Validated with esphome config 2026.10.)

          - OFF for at least 50ms

          - logger.log: "double tap -> wake Edith"
      - timing:
          - ON for at most 280ms
          - OFF for at least 350ms
        then:
          - logger.log: "single tap -> play/pause"

    name: "Touch C (rear)"
    pin: GPIO4          # XIAO D3
    threshold: 30000
    on_press:
      - lambda: |-

            ESP_LOGI("gesture", "swipe back -> volume %.1f", id(volume));
            id(first_pad) = 0;
          } else { id(first_pad) = 3; }

    then:
      - lambda: 'id(first_pad) = 0;'

10 Prescription & photochromic lens fitting

  1. Choose a frame with removable lenses (screw-closed metal rims or a full-rim acetate frame an optician can fit), thick, straight temples (≥5 mm tall) to carry pods, and spring hinges with room for a wire loop. A 3D-printed temple pair on a standard front is another option.
  2. Measure PD (pupillary distance, mono-PD per eye) and fitting height with the frame on your face — an optician does this in a minute. Do it with the pods dry-fitted so the frame sits as it will in use.
  3. Order lenses: ask the optician to cut your Rx (or plano) lenses to the frame; photochromic (“transitions”-type) lenses work fine. Ask for an anti-reflective coating — it reduces ghost images from the HUD.
  4. HUD placement vs lens: the splitter sits 3–8 mm in front of the lens, in the upper-outer quadrant, clear of the optical centre. It must not touch the lens (scratches) and must clear the lens when the frame flexes.
  5. Verify: with lenses in, check that the HUD text is readable and that your normal straight-ahead view is completely unobstructed.
Photochromic lenses darken with UV, which improves HUD contrast outdoors — but many types barely darken behind a car windscreen (UV is filtered). Polarised sunglass lenses can make some OLED/splitter combinations dim at certain head angles.

  1. Install Arduino IDE 2.
  2. File → Preferences → Additional boards manager URLs: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/espressif/arduino-esp32/gh-pages/package_esp32_index.json

Controls

Button: short press = action of the current mode; long press (≥0.7 s) = next mode. Modes: ASSIST (photo + 4 s question → answer) → TRANSLATE (6 s clip → translation) → VISION (scene labels) → MUSIC (stream) → HEART (live BPM) → NOTIFY (latest notification). Touch strip gestures work in every mode.

/*
 * EDITH Smart Glasses firmware
 * Board : Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense (Arduino-ESP32 core 3.x, board "XIAO_ESP32S3")
 * Tools : PSRAM = "OPI PSRAM", USB CDC On Boot = "Enabled", Partition = "Huge APP" or default 8MB
 *
 * Libraries (Library Manager):
 *   - Adafruit SSD1306  + Adafruit GFX Library
 *   - SparkFun MAX3010x Pulse and Proximity Sensor Library (works with MAX30101/MAX30102)
 *   - ArduinoJson (v7)
 *   (esp_camera.h, ESP_I2S.h, WiFi, HTTPClient ship with the Espressif core)
 *
 * PIN MAP (must match the build guide's master GPIO table):
 *   D0  / GPIO1  (TOUCH1) -> Touch pad A (front, nearest hinge)  copper foil
 *   D1  / GPIO2  (TOUCH2) -> Touch pad B (middle)                 copper foil
 *   D3  / GPIO4  (TOUCH4) -> Touch pad C (rear, toward ear)       copper foil
 *   D2  / GPIO3           -> MAX98357A DIN
 *   D6  / GPIO43          -> MAX98357A BCLK
 *   D7  / GPIO44          -> MAX98357A LRC (word select)
 *   D4  / GPIO5           -> I2C SDA (SSD1306 OLED 0x3C + MAX3010x 0x57)
 *   D5  / GPIO6           -> I2C SCL
 *   D8  / GPIO7           -> Push button to GND (INPUT_PULLUP)
 *   D10 / GPIO9           -> Status LED anode via 330 ohm (cathode to GND)
 *   GPIO42 / GPIO41 -> onboard PDM mic CLK / DATA (Sense board, do not wire)
 *   Camera: onboard (Sense board), pins below per Seeed wiki
 *   BAT+ / BAT- pads (underside) -> LiPo via slide switch on the + lead
 *   NOTE: D8/D9/D10 are shared with the Sense board's microSD slot (SCK/MISO/MOSI).
 *         This build does not use the SD card - leave the slot EMPTY.
 *
 * Controls:
 *   button short press  = do the action of the current mode
 *   button long press   = next mode   (>= 700 ms)
 *   touch strip: swipe forward (C->B->A) = volume up, swipe back (A->B->C) = volume down,
 *                single tap = play/pause, double tap = wake Edith (start listening)
 *   Modes: ASSIST -> TRANSLATE -> VISION(AR) -> MUSIC -> HEART -> NOTIFY
 */

// ---------------- capacitive touch strip ----------------
// ESP32-S3: touchRead() value RISES when touched (opposite of the original ESP32).
const uint8_t TOUCH_PINS[3] = {PIN_TOUCH_A, PIN_TOUCH_B, PIN_TOUCH_C};
uint32_t touchBase[3];
float    TOUCH_RATIO = 1.12f;          // touched if value > baseline * ratio (tune 1.05-1.30)
const uint16_t TAP_MAX_MS       = 280; // a tap is shorter than this
const uint16_t DOUBLE_GAP_MS    = 350; // second tap must start within this after the first ends
const uint16_t SWIPE_MAX_MS     = 700; // whole swipe must finish within this
const uint16_t SWIPE_MIN_STEP   = 15;  // pads must light up at least this far apart (ms)
const uint16_t TOUCH_DEBOUNCE   = 3;   // consecutive samples to change pad state

enum Gesture { G_NONE, G_SWIPE_FWD, G_SWIPE_BACK, G_TAP, G_DOUBLE_TAP };
float volumeGain = 0.6f;               // 0.0 - 1.0 software volume
bool  paused = false;

bool padState[3] = {false, false, false};
uint8_t padCount[3] = {0, 0, 0};
unsigned long padFirstOn[3];
bool gestureActive = false;
unsigned long gestureStart = 0;
unsigned long lastTapEnd = 0;
bool tapPending = false;

void calibrateTouch() {
  for (int p = 0; p < 3; p++) {
    uint64_t sum = 0;
    for (int i = 0; i < 32; i++) { sum += touchRead(TOUCH_PINS[p]); delay(2); }
    touchBase[p] = (uint32_t)(sum / 32);
    Serial.printf("touch pad %d (GPIO%d) baseline %u\n", p, TOUCH_PINS[p], touchBase[p]);
  }
}

// Call often (every loop / every audio chunk). Returns a gesture once when recognised.
Gesture pollTouch() {
  unsigned long now = millis();
  bool anyOn = false;
  for (int p = 0; p < 3; p++) {
    uint32_t v = touchRead(TOUCH_PINS[p]);
    bool raw = v > (uint32_t)(touchBase[p] * TOUCH_RATIO);
    if (raw != padState[p]) {
      if (++padCount[p] >= TOUCH_DEBOUNCE) {
        padState[p] = raw; padCount[p] = 0;
        if (raw) {
          if (!gestureActive) { gestureActive = true; gestureStart = now; for (int k = 0; k < 3; k++) padFirstOn[k] = 0; }
          if (padFirstOn[p] == 0) padFirstOn[p] = now;
        }
      }
    } else padCount[p] = 0;
    if (!raw && !padState[p]) {
      // slow drift compensation while untouched
      touchBase[p] = (touchBase[p] * 63 + v) / 64;
    }
    anyOn |= padState[p];
  }

  if (gestureActive && !anyOn) {                 // finger lifted: classify
    gestureActive = false;
    unsigned long dur = now - gestureStart;
    unsigned long a = padFirstOn[0], b = padFirstOn[1], c = padFirstOn[2];
    int lit = (a > 0) + (b > 0) + (c > 0);
    if (lit >= 2 && dur <= SWIPE_MAX_MS) {
      // order of first activation decides direction
      unsigned long first = 0, last = 0; int fi = -1, li = -1;
      for (int p = 0; p < 3; p++) if (padFirstOn[p]) {
        if (fi < 0 || padFirstOn[p] < first) { first = padFirstOn[p]; fi = p; }
        if (li < 0 || padFirstOn[p] >= last) { last = padFirstOn[p]; li = p; }
      }
      if (last - first >= SWIPE_MIN_STEP && fi != li) {
        tapPending = false;
        return (fi > li) ? G_SWIPE_FWD : G_SWIPE_BACK;   // C->A = forward, A->C = back
      }
    }
    if (dur <= TAP_MAX_MS) {
      if (tapPending && (gestureStart - lastTapEnd) <= DOUBLE_GAP_MS) { tapPending = false; return G_DOUBLE_TAP; }
      tapPending = true; lastTapEnd = now;
    }
  }
  if (tapPending && !gestureActive && now - lastTapEnd > DOUBLE_GAP_MS) { tapPending = false; return G_TAP; }
  return G_NONE;
}

void hud(const String& body, const char* title = nullptr) {
  if (!haveDisplay) { Serial.println(body); return; }
  display.clearDisplay();
  display.setTextColor(SSD1306_WHITE);
  display.setTextSize(1);
  display.setCursor(0, 0);
  display.print(title ? title : MODE_NAMES[mode]);
  if (beatAvg > 0) {               // BPM always in the corner when known
    display.setCursor(92, 0);
    display.printf("%3d\x03", beatAvg);
  }
  display.drawFastHLine(0, 9, 128, SSD1306_WHITE);
  display.setCursor(0, 12);
  display.setTextWrap(true);
  display.print(body.substring(0, 150));   // ~7 lines x 21 chars
  display.display();
  Serial.printf("[HUD] %s\n", body.c_str());
}

void connectWiFi() {
  if (WiFi.status() == WL_CONNECTED) return;
  hud("Connecting WiFi...", "EDITH");
  WiFi.mode(WIFI_STA);
  WiFi.begin(WIFI_SSID, WIFI_PASSWORD);
  unsigned long t0 = millis();
  while (WiFi.status() != WL_CONNECTED && millis() - t0 < 15000) { delay(250); led(!digitalRead(PIN_LED)); }
  led(false);
  if (WiFi.status() == WL_CONNECTED) hud("WiFi OK " + WiFi.localIP().toString(), "EDITH");
  else hud("WiFi failed - check SSID/pass", "EDITH");
}

// Build multipart/form-data in PSRAM and POST it. Returns HTTP body.
String postMultipart(const char* path, const char* modeName,
                     const uint8_t* jpg, size_t jpgLen,
                     const uint8_t* wav, size_t wavLen) {
  const String boundary = "----edithBoundary7MA4YWxk";
  String head;
  head += "--" + boundary + "\r\nContent-Disposition: form-data; name=\"mode\"\r\n\r\n" + modeName + "\r\n";
  head += "--" + boundary + "\r\nContent-Disposition: form-data; name=\"target_lang\"\r\n\r\n" + TRANSLATE_TO + "\r\n";
  String imgHead = "--" + boundary + "\r\nContent-Disposition: form-data; name=\"image\"; filename=\"frame.jpg\"\r\nContent-Type: image/jpeg\r\n\r\n";
  String audHead = "\r\n--" + boundary + "\r\nContent-Disposition: form-data; name=\"audio\"; filename=\"clip.wav\"\r\nContent-Type: audio/wav\r\n\r\n";
  String tail = "\r\n--" + boundary + "--\r\n";

  size_t total = head.length() + tail.length();
  if (jpg) total += imgHead.length() + jpgLen;
  if (wav) total += audHead.length() + wavLen;
  uint8_t* body = (uint8_t*)ps_malloc(total + 4);
  if (!body) return "";
  size_t p = 0;
  auto put = [&](const uint8_t* d, size_t n) { memcpy(body + p, d, n); p += n; };
  put((const uint8_t*)head.c_str(), head.length());
  if (jpg) { put((const uint8_t*)imgHead.c_str(), imgHead.length()); put(jpg, jpgLen); }
  if (wav) {
    if (!jpg) { String h = audHead.substring(2); put((const uint8_t*)h.c_str(), h.length()); }
    else put((const uint8_t*)audHead.c_str(), audHead.length());
    put(wav, wavLen);
  }
  put((const uint8_t*)tail.c_str(), tail.length());

  HTTPClient http;
  http.setTimeout(60000);
  http.begin(String(BACKEND_URL) + path);
  http.addHeader("Content-Type", "multipart/form-data; boundary=" + boundary);
  http.addHeader("X-Device-Token", DEVICE_TOKEN);
  int code = http.POST(body, p);
  String resp = code > 0 ? http.getString() : "";
  Serial.printf("POST %s -> %d (%u bytes sent)\n", path, code, (unsigned)p);
  http.end();
  free(body);
  return resp;
}

bool wakeRequested = false;

void applyGesture(Gesture g, bool inAudio) {
  switch (g) {
    case G_SWIPE_FWD:  volumeGain = min(1.0f, volumeGain + 0.1f); hud("Volume " + String((int)(volumeGain * 100)) + "%", "VOL+"); break;
    case G_SWIPE_BACK: volumeGain = max(0.0f, volumeGain - 0.1f); hud("Volume " + String((int)(volumeGain * 100)) + "%", "VOL-"); break;
    case G_TAP:        if (inAudio) { paused = !paused; hud(paused ? "Paused" : "Playing", "MEDIA"); } break;
    case G_DOUBLE_TAP: wakeRequested = true; if (inAudio) stopAudio = true; break;
    default: break;
  }
}

// Stream a 16 kHz mono 16-bit WAV from the backend to the MAX98357A.
void playUrl(const String& url) {
  if (!haveSpk || url.length() == 0) return;
  HTTPClient http;
  http.useHTTP10(true);                      // no chunked encoding -> raw PCM on the socket
  http.setTimeout(30000);
  http.begin(url.startsWith("http") ? url : String(BACKEND_URL) + url);
  http.addHeader("X-Device-Token", DEVICE_TOKEN);
  int code = http.GET();
  if (code != 200) { Serial.printf("audio GET %d\n", code); http.end(); return; }
  WiFiClient* s = http.getStreamPtr();
  static uint8_t buf[2048];
  size_t skipped = 0;                        // skip the 44-byte WAV header
  unsigned long t0 = millis();
  while (skipped < 44 && millis() - t0 < 5000) { if (s->available()) { s->read(); skipped++; } else delay(1); }
  stopAudio = false; paused = false;
  int remaining = http.getSize();            // -1 when length is unknown (music stream)
  if (remaining > 0) remaining -= 44;
  unsigned long lastData = millis();
  while ((s->connected() || s->available()) && (remaining > 0 || remaining == -1) && !stopAudio) {
    applyGesture(pollTouch(), true);
    if (digitalRead(PIN_BUTTON) == LOW) stopAudio = true;   // button press = stop
    if (paused) { delay(10); lastData = millis(); continue; }
    size_t avail = s->available();
    if (avail) {
      size_t n = s->readBytes(buf, min(avail, sizeof(buf)) & ~1U);
      int16_t* smp = (int16_t*)buf;
      for (size_t i = 0; i < n / 2; i++) smp[i] = (int16_t)(smp[i] * volumeGain);
      spk.write(buf, n);
      if (remaining > 0) remaining -= n;
      lastData = millis();
    } else {
      if (millis() - lastData > 4000) break;
      delay(1);
    }
  }
  http.end();
}

void handleReply(const String& resp) {
  if (resp.length() == 0) { hud("No reply from backend."); return; }
  JsonDocument doc;
  if (deserializeJson(doc, resp)) { hud("Bad JSON from backend."); return; }
  String text = doc["hud"] | doc["text"] | "";
  JsonArray labels = doc["labels"].as<JsonArray>();
  if (!labels.isNull() && labels.size() > 0) {     // AR-style label list
    String l;
    for (JsonVariant v : labels) { l += "> "; l += v.as<const char*>(); l += "\n"; }
    text = l;
  }
  hud(text);
  String audio = doc["audio_url"] | "";
  if (audio.length()) playUrl(audio);
}

// ---------------- mode actions ----------------
void actionAssistOrTranslate(bool translate) {
  hud(translate ? "Listening (6s)..." : "Listening (4s)...");
  led(true);
  camera_fb_t* fb = nullptr;
  if (!translate && haveCam) fb = esp_camera_fb_get();     // snapshot when you start talking
  size_t wavLen = 0;
  uint8_t* wav = mic.recordWAV(translate ? 6 : 4, &wavLen);
  led(false);
  hud("Thinking...");
  String resp = postMultipart(translate ? "/api/v1/translate" : "/api/v1/assist",
                              translate ? "translate" : "assist",
                              fb ? fb->buf : nullptr, fb ? fb->len : 0, wav, wavLen);
  if (fb) esp_camera_fb_return(fb);
  if (wav) free(wav);
  handleReply(resp);
}

void actionVision() {
  if (!haveCam) { hud("Camera not detected"); return; }
  hud("Scanning scene...");
  camera_fb_t* fb = esp_camera_fb_get();
  if (!fb) { hud("Capture failed"); return; }
  String resp = postMultipart("/api/v1/vision", "vision", fb->buf, fb->len, nullptr, 0);
  esp_camera_fb_return(fb);
  handleReply(resp);
}

void actionMusic() {
  hud("Streaming music...\n(press to stop)");
  playUrl("/api/v1/music/stream");
  hud("Music stopped.");
  delay(400);
}

void pollNotify(bool force) {
  if (!force && millis() - lastNotifyPoll < 15000) return;
  lastNotifyPoll = millis();
  if (WiFi.status() != WL_CONNECTED) return;
  HTTPClient http;
  http.setTimeout(5000);
  http.begin(String(BACKEND_URL) + "/api/v1/notify");
  http.addHeader("X-Device-Token", DEVICE_TOKEN);
  if (http.GET() == 200) {
    JsonDocument doc;
    if (!deserializeJson(doc, http.getString())) {
      String t = doc["text"] | "";
      if (t.length()) { hudLine = t; if (mode == M_NOTIFY) hud(hudLine); else hud(t, "NOTICE"); }
    }
  }
  http.end();
}

void updateHeart() {
  if (!havePulse) return;
  long ir = pulse.getIR();
  fingerOn = ir > 50000;
  if (fingerOn && checkForBeat(ir)) {
    long delta = millis() - lastBeat;
    lastBeat = millis();
    float bpm = 60.0f / (delta / 1000.0f);
    if (bpm > 30 && bpm < 220) {
      rates[rateSpot++] = (byte)bpm;
      rateSpot %= RATE_SIZE;
      int sum = 0; for (byte i = 0; i < RATE_SIZE; i++) sum += rates[i];
      beatAvg = sum / RATE_SIZE;
    }
  }
  if (!fingerOn && millis() - lastBeat > 5000) beatAvg = 0;
}

// ---------------- setup / loop ----------------
void setup() {
  Serial.begin(115200);
  delay(300);
  pinMode(PIN_BUTTON, INPUT_PULLUP);
  pinMode(PIN_LED, OUTPUT);
  led(false);

  Wire.begin(PIN_SDA, PIN_SCL, 400000);
  haveDisplay = display.begin(SSD1306_SWITCHCAPVCC, 0x3C);
  if (haveDisplay) { display.setRotation(0); display.clearDisplay(); display.display(); }
  // Mirror text for a beam-splitter optic? Use display.setRotation(2) or flip in firmware.

  havePulse = pulse.begin(Wire, I2C_SPEED_FAST);
  if (havePulse) { pulse.setup(); pulse.setPulseAmplitudeRed(0x0A); pulse.setPulseAmplitudeGreen(0); }

  haveCam = initCamera();

  mic.setPinsPdmRx(PIN_MIC_CLK, PIN_MIC_DATA);
  if (!mic.begin(I2S_MODE_PDM_RX, SAMPLE_RATE, I2S_DATA_BIT_WIDTH_16BIT, I2S_SLOT_MODE_MONO))
    Serial.println("Mic init failed");

  spk.setPins(PIN_I2S_BCLK, PIN_I2S_LRC, PIN_I2S_DIN);
  haveSpk = spk.begin(I2S_MODE_STD, SAMPLE_RATE, I2S_DATA_BIT_WIDTH_16BIT, I2S_SLOT_MODE_MONO);
  if (!haveSpk) Serial.println("Speaker I2S init failed");

  calibrateTouch();                        // keep fingers off the temple while booting
  connectWiFi();
  Serial.printf("cam=%d oled=%d pulse=%d spk=%d psram=%u\n", haveCam, haveDisplay, havePulse, haveSpk, ESP.getPsramSize());
  hud(hudLine);
}

void loop() {
  static unsigned long pressedAt = 0;
  static bool wasDown = false;
  static unsigned long lastHeartDraw = 0;

  updateHeart();
  applyGesture(pollTouch(), false);
  if (wakeRequested) { wakeRequested = false; connectWiFi(); actionAssistOrTranslate(false); }
  bool down = digitalRead(PIN_BUTTON) == LOW;
  if (down && !wasDown) pressedAt = millis();
  if (!down && wasDown) {
    unsigned long held = millis() - pressedAt;
    if (held > 30) {                           // debounce
      connectWiFi();
      if (held >= 700) {
        mode = (Mode)((mode + 1) % M_COUNT);
        hud(String("Mode: ") + MODE_NAMES[mode]);
      } else {
        switch (mode) {
          case M_ASSIST:    actionAssistOrTranslate(false); break;
          case M_TRANSLATE: actionAssistOrTranslate(true);  break;
          case M_VISION:    actionVision();                 break;
          case M_MUSIC:     actionMusic();                  break;
          case M_HEART:     hud(havePulse ? "Hold sensor still..." : "No MAX3010x found"); break;
          case M_NOTIFY:    pollNotify(true); hud(hudLine); break;
          default: break;
        }
      }
    }
  }
  wasDown = down;

  if (mode == M_HEART && millis() - lastHeartDraw > 500) {
    lastHeartDraw = millis();
    hud(fingerOn ? String("BPM: ") + (beatAvg ? String(beatAvg) : String("...")) : String("Place sensor on skin"));
  }
  if (mode != M_HEART && mode != M_MUSIC) pollNotify(false);
  delay(mode == M_HEART ? 0 : 5);
}

12 Backend server (FastAPI)

The glasses are a thin client: they POST multipart/form-data (JPEG + 16 kHz WAV) and get back JSON {text, hud, audio_url, labels}, then stream the WAV from audio_url. The backend is provider-agnostic: any OpenAI-compatible /chat/completions (vision), /audio/transcriptions (Whisper) and /audio/speech (TTS) endpoint works, or local faster-whisper / espeak-ng.

EndpointUsed byWhat it does
POST /api/v1/assistASSIST, double-tapWhisper → LLM (image + text + personality + Tesla tools) → TTS

def _pcm16_from_wav(data: bytes) -> tuple[np.ndarray, int, int]:
    """Minimal RIFF parser that tolerates streaming headers (size 0xFFFFFFFF)."""
    if data[:4] != b"RIFF":
        raise ValueError("not a WAV")
    pos, rate, ch, bits = 12, 24000, 1, 16
    while pos + 8 <= len(data):
        cid, size = data[pos:pos + 4], struct.unpack("<I", data[pos + 4:pos + 8])[0]
        if cid == b"fmt ":
            _, ch, rate, _, _, bits = struct.unpack("<HHIIHH", data[pos + 8:pos + 24])
        elif cid == b"data":
            raw = data[pos + 8:] if size in (0, 0xFFFFFFFF) else data[pos + 8:pos + 8 + size]
            if bits != 16:
                raise ValueError("only 16-bit WAV supported without ffmpeg")
            pcm = np.frombuffer(raw[: len(raw) // 2 * 2], dtype="<i2")
            return pcm, rate, ch
        pos += 8 + size + (size & 1)
    raise ValueError("no data chunk")


def to_device_wav(audio: bytes) -> bytes:
    """Convert any audio to 16 kHz mono s16 WAV for the glasses."""
    if shutil.which("ffmpeg"):
        p = subprocess.run(
            ["ffmpeg", "-loglevel", "error", "-i", "pipe:0", "-ac", "1", "-ar", str(OUT_RATE),
             "-f", "s16le", "pipe:1"], input=audio, capture_output=True, check=True)
        pcm = np.frombuffer(p.stdout, dtype="<i2")
    else:
        pcm, rate, ch = _pcm16_from_wav(audio)
        if ch > 1:
            pcm = pcm.reshape(-1, ch).mean(axis=1).astype("<i2")
        if rate != OUT_RATE and len(pcm):
            n = int(len(pcm) * OUT_RATE / rate)
            pcm = np.interp(np.linspace(0, len(pcm) - 1, n), np.arange(len(pcm)), pcm).astype("<i2")
    return wav_header(len(pcm) * 2) + pcm.tobytes()


def wav_header(data_len: int, rate: int = OUT_RATE) -> bytes:
    return (b"RIFF" + struct.pack("<I", (36 + data_len) & 0xFFFFFFFF) + b"WAVEfmt "
            + struct.pack("<IHHIIHH", 16, 1, 1, rate, rate * 2, 2, 16)
            + b"data" + struct.pack("<I", data_len & 0xFFFFFFFF))


async def speak(text: str) -> str | None:
    """Synthesize text, store it, return a relative URL the glasses can GET."""
    if not text or TTS_PROVIDER == "none":
        return None
    try:
        if TTS_PROVIDER == "espeak":
            audio = subprocess.run(["espeak-ng", "--stdout", text], capture_output=True, check=True).stdout
        else:
            async with httpx.AsyncClient(timeout=60) as c:
                r = await c.post(
                    f"{TTS_BASE_URL}/audio/speech",
                    headers=auth(TTS_API_KEY),
                    json={"model": TTS_MODEL, "voice": TTS_VOICE, "input": text, "response_format": "wav"},
                )
                r.raise_for_status()
                audio = r.content
        wav = to_device_wav(audio)
    except Exception as e:  # TTS failure should never break the text reply
        print("TTS failed:", e)
        return None
    name = f"{uuid.uuid4().hex}.wav"
    (AUDIO_DIR / name).write_bytes(wav)
    for old in sorted(AUDIO_DIR.glob("*.wav"), key=lambda p: p.stat().st_mtime)[:-30]:
        old.unlink(missing_ok=True)
    return f"/api/v1/audio/{name}"


def hud_text(text: str, limit: int = 140) -> str:
    text = re.sub(r"[*_#`>]", "", text).strip()
    return text if len(text) <= limit else text[: limit - 1] + "…"

@app.post("/api/v1/assist")
async def assist(image: UploadFile | None = File(None), audio: UploadFile | None = File(None),
                 mode: str = Form("assist"), x_device_token: str | None = Header(None)):
    check_token(x_device_token)
    jpg = await image.read() if image else None
    wav = await audio.read() if audio else b""
    said, _ = await transcribe(wav)
    if not said:
        said = "(The user pressed the button but said nothing. Briefly describe what is in front of them.)"
    messages = [{"role": "system", "content": load_personality()}, *history,
                {"role": "user", "content": user_content(said, jpg)}]
    tools = tesla.TOOLS if tesla.enabled() else None
    msg = await chat(messages, tools)
    # one round of tool calls (Tesla)
    if msg.get("tool_calls"):
        messages.append(msg)
        for call in msg["tool_calls"]:
            args = json.loads(call["function"].get("arguments") or "{}")
            result = await tesla.run_tool(call["function"]["name"], args)
            messages.append({"role": "tool", "tool_call_id": call["id"], "content": json.dumps(result)})
        msg = await chat(messages)
    reply = (msg.get("content") or "").strip() or "Done."
    history.extend([{"role": "user", "content": said}, {"role": "assistant", "content": reply}])
    return {"heard": said, "text": reply, "hud": hud_text(reply), "audio_url": await speak(reply)}

@app.get("/api/v1/music/stream")
def music_stream(track: str | None = None, x_device_token: str | None = Header(None)):
    """Streams a local file (mp3/flac/wav...) as endless 16 kHz mono WAV. Needs ffmpeg."""
    check_token(x_device_token)
    if not shutil.which("ffmpeg"):
        raise HTTPException(500, "install ffmpeg for music streaming")
    files = sorted(p for p in MUSIC_DIR.glob("*") if p.suffix.lower() in {".mp3", ".wav", ".flac", ".ogg", ".m4a"})
    if track:
        files = [p for p in files if track.lower() in p.name.lower()] or files
    if not files:
        raise HTTPException(404, f"put audio files in {MUSIC_DIR}")

    def gen():
        yield wav_header(0xFFFFFFFF - 36)    # unknown length
        for f in files:
            proc = subprocess.Popen(["ffmpeg", "-loglevel", "error", "-re", "-i", str(f), "-ac", "1",
                                     "-ar", str(OUT_RATE), "-f", "s16le", "pipe:1"], stdout=subprocess.PIPE)
            try:
                while chunk := proc.stdout.read(4096):
                    yield chunk
            finally:
                proc.kill()

    return StreamingResponse(gen(), media_type="audio/wav")

13 Feature recipes

1 · Heads-up display

HW: SSD1306 OLED + lens + beam-splitter (see HUD). SW: hud() in the sketch draws a title bar (mode + BPM) and ~7 lines of wrapped text.

Build: wire I²C → flash → align optic → mirror text if needed.

2 · Instant translation

Flow: TRANSLATE mode → 6 s PDM recording → /api/v1/translate → Whisper transcribes & detects language → LLM translates into TRANSLATE_TO → HUD shows [lang] text, TTS speaks it.

Build: set TRANSLATE_TO in the sketch; any Whisper-compatible STT. It's turn-based (press, listen, translate), not continuous simultaneous interpretation.

3 · Music playback

HW: MAX98357A + speaker / bone conductor. SW: MUSIC mode streams /api/v1/music/stream (ffmpeg converts your files to 16 kHz mono).

The ESP32-S3 has Bluetooth LE 5 only — no Bluetooth Classic, so no A2DP; the glasses cannot act as normal Bluetooth headphones. Music comes over Wi-Fi from the backend. 16 kHz mono is “podcast quality”, not hi-fi.

4 · Text display / notifications

POST /api/v1/notify {"text":"…"} from an iOS Shortcut, Tasker, Home Assistant or a script; the glasses poll every 15 s and pop it on the HUD (NOTIFY mode keeps the last one).

5 · “AR” scene labels

VISION mode sends a VGA JPEG; the LLM returns ≤4 labels (left-to-right) + one sentence; the HUD lists them with > bullets.

This is a 2D text overlay. There is no SLAM, depth or world-anchoring — labels don't stick to objects as you move your head, and the camera's view doesn't exactly match your eye's.

6 · Grok connection

Set LLM_BASE_URL=https://api.x.ai/v1, LLM_API_KEY=xai-… and a vision-capable model (the xAI docs list grok-4.7 as the current flagship on 2026-10-22). The backend calls the OpenAI-compatible /chat/completions endpoint.

xAI's docs now label Chat Completions “Legacy” and recommend their Responses API; Chat Completions is used here because every provider supports it.

7 · Custom AI

Edit prompts/personality.md (re-read on every request) and swap providers via .env: OpenAI (https://api.openai.com/v1), a local Ollama server (http://localhost:11434/v1) or any OpenAI-compatible gateway.

8 · Heart-rate on HUD

MAX3010x on I²C; BPM appears top-right on every HUD screen once a beat is detected; HEART mode shows it large. See Heart-rate.

9 · Tesla control

Say “lock the car”, “turn on climate”, “honk”, “flash the lights” or “what's my battery?” in ASSIST mode. The LLM calls tesla_command/tesla_battery tools; the backend executes via Fleet API or BLE. See Fleet API / BLE.

+ · Touch gestures

Swipe = volume, tap = play/pause, double-tap = wake Edith. See Touch strip.

14 Personality prompt — Edith

The backend loads everything between <!-- PROMPT START --> and <!-- PROMPT END --> in prompts/personality.md as the system prompt. Tips for writing your own:

  • Budget for the medium: spoken through a tiny speaker and a 21-character-wide HUD → 1–2 sentences, no markdown, no lists, no emoji.
  • Explain the sensors: first-person camera, may be blurry; “this/that” means what's in view.
  • Give it a voice: one or two adjectives (“dry, light wit”) beat a paragraph of backstory.

# Edith — personality prompt

The backend sends everything between the `PROMPT START` / `PROMPT END` markers as the
system prompt (see `PERSONALITY_FILE` in `backend/.env`). Edit freely; restart not required —
the file is re-read on every request.

## How to customise
1. **Name & voice** – change the first line. Match `TTS_VOICE` to the vibe.
2. **Length budget** – replies are spoken through a tiny speaker and shown on a 128×64 HUD
   (~7 lines × 21 chars). Keep "1–2 sentences, under 30 words" unless you add a longer HUD.
3. **Wit dial** – "dry, light wit" is a good default; say "no jokes" for a strictly professional assistant.
4. **Context the model can't see** – tell it about the camera (first-person view, may be blurry,
   tilted, or a lens-reflection), the HUD, and what tools exist (Tesla).
5. **Safety rules** – keep the driving / car-command rules; tool calls run real actions.
6. **Language** – add "Always answer in <language>" for a non-English default.
Test changes by pushing a sample via `curl` (see the guide's backend section).

15 Tesla Fleet API (cloud)

Facts below were checked against Tesla's Fleet API docs on 2026-10-22. Commands must be signed with your app's virtual key: Tesla's docs state that unsigned commands are rejected by the vehicle, and that the Vehicle Command Proxy (tesla-http-proxy) exposes the same REST endpoints and signs them for you. (The proxy is “not required for most business vehicles and pre-2021 S and X”.)

  1. Register an app at developer.tesla.com: set the allowed origin (your domain), redirect URI and scopes (e.g. openid offline_access user_data vehicle_device_data vehicle_cmds vehicle_charging_cmds). Note the client ID/secret. Fleet API usage is billed — read “Billing and Limits” in the docs.
  2. Create the key pair (vehicles only support prime256v1):
    openssl ecparam -name prime256v1 -genkey -noout -out private-key.pem
    openssl ec -in private-key.pem -pubout -out public-key.pem
    Keep private-key.pem secret (it goes to the proxy as fleet-key.pem).
  3. Host the public key permanently at https://<your-domain>/.well-known/appspecific/com.tesla.3p.public-key.pem. On GitHub Pages add an empty .nojekyll file so the .well-known folder is published.
  4. Register the partner account: get a partner token (grant_type=client_credentials at https://fleet-auth.prd.vn.cloud.tesla.com/oauth2/v3/token with audience = your region's Fleet API base URL), then POST /api/1/partner_accounts with {"domain":"your-domain"}; verify with GET /api/1/partner_accounts/public_key?domain=…. python tesla_auth.py partner-register does all three.
  5. User OAuth (third-party token): python tesla_auth.py login-url prints the https://auth.tesla.com/oauth2/v3/authorize?... link; sign in, copy code from the redirect, then python tesla_auth.py exchange <code>. Refresh tokens are single-use (always store the new one) and expire after 3 months — tesla.py handles this.
  6. Add the virtual key to the car: open https://tesla.com/_ak/<your-domain> on the phone with the Tesla app and approve. Remove it any time from the car's Locks screen.
  7. Run the proxy (from teslamotors/vehicle-command):
    tesla-http-proxy -tls-key config/tls-key.pem -cert config/tls-cert.pem -key-file config/fleet-key.pem -port 4443
    (or the tesla/vehicle-command Docker image). The proxy requires TLS; point TESLA_PROXY_CA at its cert.
  8. In .env: TESLA_MODE=fleet, TESLA_VIN, TESLA_CLIENT_ID, TESLA_PROXY_URL=https://localhost:4443. Test: “Edith, flash the lights.”

"""
Tesla integration for EDITH: LLM tool-calls -> Fleet API (via tesla-http-proxy) or BLE (tesla-control).

Fleet API facts used here (developer.tesla.com/docs/fleet-api, checked 2026-10-22):
  * Vehicle commands: POST /api/1/vehicles/{vin}/command/<name>  e.g. door_lock, door_unlock,
    auto_conditioning_start, auto_conditioning_stop, honk_horn, flash_lights
  * Vehicle data:     GET  /api/1/vehicles/{vin}/vehicle_data   (charge_state.battery_level)
  * Wake:             POST /api/1/vehicles/{vin}/wake_up
  * Commands must be signed with your virtual key -> send them through Tesla's Vehicle Command
    Proxy (tesla-http-proxy). The proxy exposes the same REST paths and forwards other calls.
  * Tokens: https://fleet-auth.prd.vn.cloud.tesla.com/oauth2/v3/token (refresh tokens are single use).
BLE mode shells out to `tesla-control -ble ...` from github.com/teslamotors/vehicle-command and needs
the backend host (e.g. a Raspberry Pi) within Bluetooth range of the car.
"""
from __future__ import annotations

import asyncio
import json
import os
import shutil
import time
from pathlib import Path

import httpx

MODE = os.getenv("TESLA_MODE", "off")                 # off | fleet | ble
VIN = os.getenv("TESLA_VIN", "")
PROXY_URL = os.getenv("TESLA_PROXY_URL", "https://localhost:4443").rstrip("/")
PROXY_CA = os.getenv("TESLA_PROXY_CA", "")             # path to the proxy's tls-cert.pem
CLIENT_ID = os.getenv("TESLA_CLIENT_ID", "")
TOKEN_FILE = Path(os.getenv("TESLA_TOKEN_FILE", str(Path(__file__).with_name("tesla_tokens.json"))))
AUTH_URL = "https://fleet-auth.prd.vn.cloud.tesla.com/oauth2/v3/token"
ALLOW_UNLOCK = os.getenv("TESLA_ALLOW_UNLOCK", "false").lower() == "true"
BLE_KEY_FILE = os.getenv("TESLA_BLE_KEY_FILE", "")     # private key paired via add-key-request

# intent -> (Fleet API command, tesla-control BLE command)
COMMANDS = {
    "lock":         ("door_lock", "lock"),
    "unlock":       ("door_unlock", "unlock"),
    "climate_on":   ("auto_conditioning_start", "climate-on"),
    "climate_off":  ("auto_conditioning_stop", "climate-off"),
    "honk":         ("honk_horn", "honk"),
    "flash_lights": ("flash_lights", "flash-lights"),
}

TOOLS = [
    {"type": "function", "function": {
        "name": "tesla_command",
        "description": "Control the user's Tesla. Use only when the user clearly asks.",
        "parameters": {"type": "object", "properties": {
            "action": {"type": "string", "enum": list(COMMANDS)}}, "required": ["action"]}}},
    {"type": "function", "function": {
        "name": "tesla_battery",
        "description": "Get the Tesla's battery level and range.",
        "parameters": {"type": "object", "properties": {}}}},
]


def enabled() -> bool:
    return MODE in ("fleet", "ble") and bool(VIN)


# ------------------------------------------------------------------ tokens
def _load_tokens() -> dict:
    if TOKEN_FILE.exists():
        return json.loads(TOKEN_FILE.read_text())
    return {"access_token": os.getenv("TESLA_ACCESS_TOKEN", ""),
            "refresh_token": os.getenv("TESLA_REFRESH_TOKEN", ""), "expires_at": 0}


async def access_token() -> str:
    tok = _load_tokens()
    if tok.get("access_token") and tok.get("expires_at", 0) > time.time() + 60:
        return tok["access_token"]
    if not tok.get("refresh_token"):
        return tok.get("access_token", "")
    async with httpx.AsyncClient(timeout=30) as c:
        r = await c.post(AUTH_URL, data={"grant_type": "refresh_token", "client_id": CLIENT_ID,
                                         "refresh_token": tok["refresh_token"]})
        r.raise_for_status()
        j = r.json()
    # refresh tokens are single-use: always persist the new one
    tok = {"access_token": j["access_token"], "refresh_token": j.get("refresh_token", tok["refresh_token"]),
           "expires_at": time.time() + int(j.get("expires_in", 3600))}
    TOKEN_FILE.write_text(json.dumps(tok))
    return tok["access_token"]


# ------------------------------------------------------------------ fleet (via proxy)
async def _fleet(method: str, path: str, body: dict | None = None) -> dict:
    headers = {"Authorization": f"Bearer {await access_token()}", "Content-Type": "application/json"}
    async with httpx.AsyncClient(timeout=40, verify=PROXY_CA or True) as c:
        r = await c.request(method, f"{PROXY_URL}{path}", headers=headers, json=body)
        try:
            return {"status": r.status_code, **r.json()}
        except ValueError:
            return {"status": r.status_code, "body": r.text[:200]}


async def _fleet_wake() -> None:
    await _fleet("POST", f"/api/1/vehicles/{VIN}/wake_up")
    for _ in range(10):  # wait until online
        data = await _fleet("GET", f"/api/1/vehicles/{VIN}/vehicle_data")
        if data.get("status") == 200:
            return
        await asyncio.sleep(3)


# ------------------------------------------------------------------ BLE (tesla-control)
async def _ble(*args: str) -> dict:
    exe = shutil.which("tesla-control")
    if not exe:
        return {"ok": False, "error": "tesla-control not installed on backend host"}
    cmd = [exe, "-ble", "-vin", VIN]
    if BLE_KEY_FILE:
        cmd += ["-key-file", BLE_KEY_FILE]
    proc = await asyncio.create_subprocess_exec(*cmd, *args, stdout=asyncio.subprocess.PIPE,
                                                stderr=asyncio.subprocess.PIPE)
    out, err = await asyncio.wait_for(proc.communicate(), timeout=40)
    return {"ok": proc.returncode == 0, "output": out.decode()[-500:], "error": err.decode()[-300:]}


# ------------------------------------------------------------------ tool dispatcher
async def run_tool(name: str, args: dict) -> dict:
    if not enabled():
        return {"ok": False, "error": "Tesla integration is off"}
    if name == "tesla_command":
        action = args.get("action", "")
        if action not in COMMANDS:
            return {"ok": False, "error": f"unknown action {action}"}
        if action == "unlock" and not ALLOW_UNLOCK:
            return {"ok": False, "error": "unlock disabled (set TESLA_ALLOW_UNLOCK=true)"}
        fleet_cmd, ble_cmd = COMMANDS[action]
        if MODE == "ble":
            return await _ble(ble_cmd)
        await _fleet_wake()
        return await _fleet("POST", f"/api/1/vehicles/{VIN}/command/{fleet_cmd}", {})
    if name == "tesla_battery":
        if MODE == "ble":
            return await _ble("state", "charge")   # see guide: verify with `tesla-control -h`
        await _fleet_wake()
        data = await _fleet("GET", f"/api/1/vehicles/{VIN}/vehicle_data")
        cs = (data.get("response") or {}).get("charge_state") or {}
        return {"ok": data.get("status") == 200, "battery_level": cs.get("battery_level"),
                "battery_range_miles": cs.get("battery_range"), "charging_state": cs.get("charging_state")}
    return {"ok": False, "error": f"unknown tool {name}"}

"""
One-time Tesla Fleet API setup helper.

  python tesla_auth.py partner-register     # partner token + POST /api/1/partner_accounts
  python tesla_auth.py login-url            # prints the user authorization URL
  python tesla_auth.py exchange <code>      # trades the callback ?code= for tokens -> tesla_tokens.json

Endpoints from developer.tesla.com/docs/fleet-api (Partner Tokens, Third-Party Tokens,
Partner Endpoints), checked 2026-10-22.
"""
import json
import os
import secrets
import sys
import time
import urllib.parse
from pathlib import Path

import httpx
from dotenv import load_dotenv

load_dotenv()
CLIENT_ID = os.environ.get("TESLA_CLIENT_ID", "")
CLIENT_SECRET = os.environ.get("TESLA_CLIENT_SECRET", "")
REDIRECT_URI = os.environ.get("TESLA_REDIRECT_URI", "https://your-domain.example/auth/callback")
DOMAIN = os.environ.get("TESLA_APP_DOMAIN", "your-domain.example")
AUDIENCE = os.environ.get("TESLA_FLEET_BASE", "https://fleet-api.prd.na.vn.cloud.tesla.com")
SCOPES = "openid offline_access user_data vehicle_device_data vehicle_cmds vehicle_charging_cmds"
TOKEN_URL = "https://fleet-auth.prd.vn.cloud.tesla.com/oauth2/v3/token"
TOKEN_FILE = Path(os.environ.get("TESLA_TOKEN_FILE", str(Path(__file__).with_name("tesla_tokens.json"))))


def partner_register():
    r = httpx.post(TOKEN_URL, data={"grant_type": "client_credentials", "client_id": CLIENT_ID,
                                    "client_secret": CLIENT_SECRET, "scope": SCOPES.replace("offline_access ", ""),
                                    "audience": AUDIENCE})
    r.raise_for_status()
    partner = r.json()["access_token"]
    r = httpx.post(f"{AUDIENCE}/api/1/partner_accounts", json={"domain": DOMAIN},
                   headers={"Authorization": f"Bearer {partner}"})
    print("register:", r.status_code, r.text[:400])
    r = httpx.get(f"{AUDIENCE}/api/1/partner_accounts/public_key", params={"domain": DOMAIN},
                  headers={"Authorization": f"Bearer {partner}"})
    print("public_key check:", r.status_code, r.text[:400])


def login_url():
    q = {"response_type": "code", "client_id": CLIENT_ID, "redirect_uri": REDIRECT_URI,
         "scope": SCOPES, "state": secrets.token_hex(16), "prompt_missing_scopes": "true"}
    print("https://auth.tesla.com/oauth2/v3/authorize?" + urllib.parse.urlencode(q))
    print(f"\nAfter approving, pair the virtual key in the Tesla app: https://tesla.com/_ak/{DOMAIN}")


def exchange(code: str):
    r = httpx.post(TOKEN_URL, data={"grant_type": "authorization_code", "client_id": CLIENT_ID,
                                    "client_secret": CLIENT_SECRET, "code": code, "audience": AUDIENCE,
                                    "redirect_uri": REDIRECT_URI})
    r.raise_for_status()
    j = r.json()
    TOKEN_FILE.write_text(json.dumps({"access_token": j["access_token"], "refresh_token": j.get("refresh_token"),
                                      "expires_at": time.time() + int(j.get("expires_in", 3600))}))
    print("saved", TOKEN_FILE)


if __name__ == "__main__":
    cmd = sys.argv[1] if len(sys.argv) > 1 else ""
    if cmd == "partner-register":
        partner_register()
    elif cmd == "login-url":
        login_url()
    elif cmd == "exchange" and len(sys.argv) > 2:
        exchange(sys.argv[2])

16 Tesla BLE (local, no cloud)

Teslas accept the same signed commands over Bluetooth LE. The official CLI tesla-control (in vehicle-command/cmd/tesla-control) runs on Linux/macOS (not Windows, per its README). The backend host must be within BLE range of the car (e.g. a Raspberry Pi in the garage) — the glasses talk to the backend over Wi-Fi as usual.

  1. Install Go, clone teslamotors/vehicle-command, run go get ./... and go install ./... (puts tesla-keygen, tesla-control, tesla-http-proxy on your PATH).
  2. export TESLA_KEY_NAME=$(whoami), export TESLA_VIN=<VIN>, then tesla-keygen create > public_key.pem (private key goes into the system keyring).
  3. Pair via NFC card tap: sit in the car with Bluetooth on and your key card handy, run tesla-control -ble add-key-request public_key.pem owner cloud_key, then tap the NFC key card on the centre console when prompted and confirm on the touchscreen.
  4. Test: tesla-control -ble lock. Then set TESLA_MODE=ble and TESLA_VIN in .env (and TESLA_BLE_KEY_FILE if you exported the key to a file instead of the keyring).

ESP32 BLE projects (verified repos)

  • yoziru/tesla-ble — C++ library implementing the vehicle-command BLE protocol for embedded systems.
  • yoziru/esphome-tesla-ble — ESPHome component (pair key, wake, charging, lock/unlock, climate, sensors). Its README says the generic package targets classic ESP32 and that S3 boards need a matching board package.
  • PedroKTFC/esphome-tesla-ble — a community fork of the ESPHome approach.
Running Tesla BLE on the glasses' ESP32-S3 alongside camera, Wi-Fi, I²S and the HUD is possible in theory but would need a big port of those libraries and RAM juggling. The practical route is a separate ESP32 (or the Pi) near the car running one of the projects above, with the backend relaying commands (e.g. through Home Assistant).

17 Safety & troubleshooting

Safety

  • LiPo next to your head: protected cell only, no crushed/swollen cells, insulate every joint, don't charge while wearing it at first, unplug if anything gets warm.
  • The ESP32-S3 + camera runs hot; use Seeed's heat sink and insulate the pod from your skin.
  • Never let the HUD block your central vision, and don't use HUD/AI features while driving.
  • Recording people may be restricted where you live — keep the privacy LED visible.
  • Tesla: keep TESLA_ALLOW_UNLOCK=false unless you really need it; protect DEVICE_TOKEN, private keys and tesla_tokens.json.

Troubleshooting

  • No COM port / upload fails: hold BOOT while plugging in; use a data (not charge-only) cable.
  • Camera init failed: PSRAM must be “OPI PSRAM”; re-seat the Sense board and camera ribbon.
  • oled=0 / pulse=0: check SDA=D4, SCL=D5, 3V3/GND; run an I²C scanner (expect 0x3C and 0x57).
  • No sound: BCLK=D6, LRC=D7, DIN=D2; check speaker on amp +/−; MUSIC mode needs ffmpeg + files in backend/music/.
  • Phantom touches: raise TOUCH_RATIO, shorten pad wires, re-boot with fingers off the strip.
  • 401 from backend: DEVICE_TOKEN differs between sketch and .env.
